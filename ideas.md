# Design Direction

The product should feel like an elegant creative atelier combined with a precise release-control system. It should be restrained, editorial, and trustworthy rather than resembling a dense ad-manager dashboard.

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| Palette | Warm porcelain surfaces, deep ink typography, muted stone borders, and a concentrated electric-violet accent reserved for creation and approval actions |
| Typography | Instrument Serif for expressive display headlines; Geist or Manrope for interface text and data |
| Layout | Asymmetric public landing page; authenticated application with a narrow sculpted navigation rail and generous content canvas |
| Surfaces | Soft tonal elevation, hairline borders, subtle inner highlights, and large calm radii; avoid excessive glassmorphism |
| Imagery | Editorial creative-contact-sheet compositions, controlled crops, visible format labels, and realistic ad-safe areas |
| Motion | Sub-300ms transitions, subtle staggered entrances, responsive pressed states, and no decorative motion on frequent controls |
| Trust cues | Approval status is always visible; consequential actions use a review drawer, frozen payload summary, typed confirmation, and clear actor/time attribution |

The core visual motif is the **frame**: assets, briefs, and generated ads sit in carefully proportioned frames that communicate their status through a slim edge treatment rather than loud badges. The activity log uses a quiet chronological ledger treatment. Purple indicates AI creation, green indicates human approval, and amber indicates a pending decision; red is reserved for rejection or failure.
